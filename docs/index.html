Today's market was driven by two massive forces pulling in opposite directions: inside tech, Microsoft soared while Meta crashed, showing that not all AI investments are created equal. Outside of tech, the situation was darker — the U.S. struck Iran and Iran threatened same-day retaliation, pushing gold to record highs and sending long-term interest rates sharply higher, which puts pressure on stock prices broadly.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • Iran threatens immediate retaliation after U.S. strikes — this is the most dangerous overnight event; a military escalation could spike oil prices and crash global markets when Asia opens.
  • Microsoft +9% vs. Meta -9% — this split tells us the AI boom rewards companies selling the "picks and shovels" (cloud infrastructure) and punishes those still searching for AI revenue (social media apps).
  • 30-year Treasury yield hits 5.14% — when the government's long-term borrowing cost rises this fast, it acts like gravity on stock prices, especially for growth companies, because future profits are worth less today.
💡 Concepto del día

Today's key concept is the **Yield Curve Bear Steepener** — this happens when long-term interest rates rise FASTER than short-term rates, widening the gap between them. Think of it like a seesaw tilting: the short end (3-month T-bill at 3.66%) stays relatively low while the long end (30-year at 5.14%) shoots up. This is the most dangerous configuration for stocks because it signals that bond investors fear future inflation or fiscal problems, demanding higher compensation to lend money for 30 years — and that higher "price of money" directly competes with stock returns.

👀 Para mañana

The single most critical watch item for the next 24-48 hours is whether Iran follows through on its retaliation threat — any military action against U.S. bases, Gulf shipping, or Saudi oil infrastructure will immediately reprice oil $10+ higher and trigger a VIX spike above 25. Additionally, watch for Alphabet's earnings report, which will either confirm Microsoft's cloud AI growth story or spread Meta's fear across the entire big tech sector.
