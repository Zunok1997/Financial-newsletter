  • WTI crude oil surged over 8% in a single day to $108 — this matters because expensive oil acts like a tax on every business and consumer, making it harder for the Federal Reserve to cut interest rates even if the economy slows.
  • Meta missed on user growth despite strong ad revenue — this matters because it reveals a ceiling on how many people are engaging with Meta's apps, which is the foundation of its entire business model long-term.
💡 Concepto del día

**Stagflation** — today's oil shock makes this word critically important to understand. Stagflation occurs when prices rise (inflation) at the same time that economic growth slows — it is the worst of both worlds because the medicine for one problem (raising rates to fight inflation) makes the other problem (slow growth) worse. Think of it like trying to cure a fever with ice while the patient already has hypothermia — the treatments conflict. The Fed currently faces this exact dilemma: oil at $108 screams "raise rates," but a slowing economy whispers "cut rates."

👀 Para mañana

Thursday's Core PCE inflation report is the single most important data release of the week — if it comes in above the 2.6% consensus expectation (which oil prices suggest is possible), expect the 10Y Treasury to test 4.50%, the 30Y to break above 5%, and growth stocks to reprice lower as rate cut hopes evaporate further. Watch also for any OPEC+ or geopolitical commentary that either confirms or explains today's extraordinary oil spike, as that context will determine whether $108 crude is a one-day shock or the beginning of a new range.
