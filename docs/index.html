💡 Concepto del día

**Bear Steepener**: Today the yield curve "steepened" because long-term interest rates (30Y at 5.10%) rose faster than short-term rates (3M at 3.70%). Think of it like a seesaw tilting toward the long end — this happens when investors fear future inflation or government borrowing, not immediate recession. It's actually bad news for bonds and can squeeze bank profit margins over time.
