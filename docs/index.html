📋 Resumen del día

Today's market is sending a complex, somewhat contradictory signal — U.S. stock indexes are barely positive, but underneath the surface, oil is surging past $100 a barrel, bond yields are rising, and volatility is ticking up. This "everything going up at once" combination of oil, yields, and the dollar is the early signature of a stagflationary environment, where prices rise but growth slows — the most challenging backdrop for investors because there are fewer safe hiding places.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • WTI crude oil above $100 — this directly raises costs for businesses and consumers, threatens corporate profit margins, and keeps the Fed from cutting interest rates anytime soon
  • Trump-Xi summit — a trade deal would be one of the biggest positive surprises of 2026, while failure could reignite tariff fears and hit global markets hard when Asia opens Wednesday
  • 30Y Treasury approaching 5.00% — this is a critical psychological level; if long-term borrowing costs break above 5%, it forces pension funds and insurers to rebalance, which can trigger a broader sell-off in stocks and bonds simultaneously
💡 Concepto del día

**Stagflation** is when an economy experiences rising prices (inflation) AND slow or stagnant growth at the same time — the worst of both worlds, because normally the tools to fight one problem make the other worse. Think of it like a car that's overheating AND running out of gas simultaneously: stepping on the gas (stimulus) makes the overheating worse, but cooling the engine (rate hikes) risks stalling completely. Today's combination of $100+ oil, rising Treasury yields, and flat-to-slow equity markets is the early warning signal investors watch for stagflation risk.

Additionally, keep an eye on whether the 10Y Treasury yield breaks decisively above 4.50%, which would be the technical trigger for a broader repricing of growth stocks.
