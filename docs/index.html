📋 Resumen del día

Today was a strong broad-based rally driven by one dominant theme: the proposed Iran-U.S. deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. Stocks surged globally, oil crashed, gold hit new highs, and bond yields fell — a rare combination that tells us markets are simultaneously celebrating geopolitical peace AND hedging against the uncertainty of whether this deal will actually hold.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • Iran-U.S. Hormuz deal — it's the single biggest catalyst today, directly causing oil's 3% crash and powering the global equity rally by reducing war risk premiums
  • Gold at $4,235 (+3.55%) rising alongside stocks — this is unusual and signals smart money is hedging against the deal falling apart or inflation staying sticky even if peace holds
  • USD/JPY at 160.12 — the Japanese yen is dangerously weak and if Japan's central bank intervenes suddenly to strengthen it, global markets could experience a sharp shock because trillions of dollars of "carry trades" depend on yen staying weak
💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **Geopolitical Risk Premium** — the extra price investors demand (or pay) because of uncertainty caused by wars, sanctions, or political instability. Think of it like paying extra for car insurance when you live in a high-accident neighborhood. When the Iran deal was announced, the geopolitical risk premium in oil prices collapsed, which is why crude fell 3% even though everything else was going up — the "insurance cost" was suddenly removed from oil prices.

👀 Para mañana

Watch for any official confirmation or denial of the Iran deal signing over the weekend — if Trump or Iranian officials walk back the agreement, Monday's open could see oil spike and equities gap lower sharply. Also monitor any BOJ statements on the yen, as USD/JPY at 160 is historically the level where Japanese authorities have previously intervened.
