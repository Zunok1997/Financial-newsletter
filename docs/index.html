💡 Concepto del día

**The VIX Term Structure** — Today's VIX is 16.01 (calm), but the 3-Month VIX is 18.57 (more worried). Think of it like weather: today is sunny, but the forecast for next month shows storms. When short-term calm and longer-term fear diverge like this, experienced investors use it as a signal to buy insurance (hedges) while it's still cheap — before the storm arrives.
