💡 Concepto del día

**VIX Term Structure (Spot vs. 3-Month)** — Today, the VIX spot is at 15.77 but the 3-month VIX is at 18.66, meaning markets are *more* calm today than they expect to be in 3 months. Think of it like calm weather on a sunny Monday while the weekend forecast shows storms — everything feels fine now, but the options market is quietly buying insurance for what comes next. When this "inversion" exists, experienced investors use the window of current calm to put on hedges before the storm arrives.
