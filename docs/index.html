💡 Concepto del día

**Yield Curve** — think of it like a timeline of trust. When you lend money to the government for 3 months, you earn 3.62%; for 10 years, you earn 4.48%. Normally longer = higher because you're taking more risk waiting. Today, bonds are rallying (yields falling) because investors think the jobs report will be weak and the Fed will cut rates — so they're locking in today's higher yields before that happens. It's like buying a fixed-rate mortgage before rates drop.
