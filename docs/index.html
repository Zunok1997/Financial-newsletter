💡 Concepto del día

**Stagflation** is when the economy faces *both* rising prices (inflation) *and* slow economic growth at the same time — it's the worst of both worlds for policymakers because the usual tools don't work well. Think of it like a car that's overheating AND running out of gas simultaneously: you can't just press the accelerator (stimulus) or slam the brakes (rate hikes) without making one problem worse. Today, oil at $100 while Target reports weakening consumer spending is a classic early stagflation signal worth monitoring carefully.
