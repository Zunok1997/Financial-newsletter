💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **VIX Term Structure** — the difference between how much fear is priced for *right now* versus *three months from now*. Think of it like car insurance: if your daily driving feels safe but you're worried about a road trip next month, you'd pay more for future coverage. Today, spot VIX at 16 says "markets feel calm now," but the 3-month VIX at 19.43 says "professional investors are paying up to protect against something bigger coming." When this gap widens sharply — as it did today with a +4.13% jump in 3M VIX — it's one of the most reliable signals that institutional investors see risks the headlines haven't caught up to yet.
