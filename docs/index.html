📋 Resumen del día

Today was one of the most instructive sessions of 2026 — the market delivered a masterclass in separating winners from losers in the AI economy, with Amazon soaring 12% while Apple dropped 7% on the same day, all against a backdrop of a Fed rate decision that gave stocks a green light to rally broadly. The deeper story, however, is more complex: bond markets are pushing long-term yields higher (30Y at 5.21%), oil prices are surging because of an active Iran war, and Bitcoin is falling even as stocks celebrate — all signals that not everything in this rally is as clean as the headline numbers suggest.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • Amazon +12% vs Apple -7% — this tells you the market has moved from "AI hype" to "AI proof," and companies that cannot show real revenue from AI are now being penalized, not just overlooked
  • Iran war pushes oil to $84-$90 — an active geopolitical conflict is now an embedded inflation driver, which is why bond yields keep rising even when the Fed is trying to pause rate hikes
  • USD/JPY drops 1.98% to 160 — the Japanese yen strengthening sharply is a warning signal for global markets because a lot of investors borrowed cheap yen to buy risky assets, and if the yen keeps rising, they may be forced to sell those assets quickly
💡 Concepto del día

Today's Amazon vs. Apple divergence is a perfect illustration of "earnings quality discrimination" — the idea that in mature bull markets, investors stop buying sectors or themes and start demanding proof of profit. Think of it like a restaurant: early in its life, people go because it's new and exciting (the "AI hype" phase); later, people only return if the food is actually good (the "AI monetization" phase). We are firmly in the second phase now, and companies that cannot put AI dollars on their income statement are going to be punished even if their underlying business is strong.
