💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is the **Carry Trade**. A carry trade is when investors borrow money in a low-interest currency (like the Japanese yen at near-zero rates) and invest it in higher-yielding assets elsewhere — pocketing the difference. Think of it like borrowing from a friend who charges you 0% interest and putting that money in a savings account paying 4.5%. The danger: if the yen suddenly strengthens (as it did today, -0.78%), the cost of repaying that loan in yen terms jumps, forcing investors to sell their investments quickly — which is why a strong yen can cause market selloffs thousands of miles away.
