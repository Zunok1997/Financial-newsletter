  • U.S.-Iran Peace Deal — this is the biggest geopolitical event in years; it reshapes oil prices, defense spending, and global risk appetite in one stroke
  • SpaceX surpasses $2 trillion — the space economy just became a legitimate asset class, and every related stock will be re-priced in the coming weeks
  • Gold surging while stocks also rise — this rare "everything up except oil" signal means markets are worried about dollar weakness and government debt, not just celebrating peace
💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is the **Geopolitical Risk Premium** — the extra price investors pay for assets (like oil or defense stocks) because of the possibility of war or conflict. Think of it like paying extra for travel insurance before a hurricane season: when the storm risk disappears, the insurance price drops immediately. Today, the Iran deal "canceled the hurricane warning," and oil fell over 5% as that risk premium evaporated almost instantly.
