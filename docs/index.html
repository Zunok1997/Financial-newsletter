💡 Concepto del día

**Bearish Divergence** — This is when an asset fails to rally (or actually falls) even when there's a very good reason it should go up. Think of it like this: if your favorite sports team is playing their weakest opponent and still loses, that tells you something important about their underlying condition. Today, oil had every reason to spike — Middle East tensions, Strait of Hormuz fears — but it fell anyway. That's a bearish divergence, and experienced traders treat it as a red flag that the asset is fundamentally weak beneath the surface.
