💡 Concepto del día

**VIX Term Structure Inversion** — Today, the short-term fear gauge (VIX) is at 17.12, but the 3-month VIX is at 20.00. This means the market is calm *right now* but expects more turbulence *soon*. Think of it like a weather forecast: today is sunny, but meteorologists are warning of storms next month. Smart investors use this signal to buy cheap insurance (hedges) while everyone else is relaxed.
