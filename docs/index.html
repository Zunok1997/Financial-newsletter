💡 Concepto del día

**The VIX Term Structure** — today's VIX is 18.37 (current fear level, relatively calm) but the VIX 3-Month is 20.54 (future fear level, higher). Think of it like weather forecasting: today is mild, but professional meteorologists are predicting a storm in the next 90 days. When the future VIX is higher than the current VIX (called "contango"), it means sophisticated options traders are paying extra money to insure their portfolios against future turbulence — they see risks on the horizon that the day-to-day market price doesn't yet fully reflect. This is a yellow flag, not a red siren, but smart investors notice it.
