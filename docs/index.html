💡 Concepto del día

**Geopolitical Risk Premium** is the extra price investors demand (or pay for commodities) to compensate for the uncertainty created by wars, military conflicts, or political instability. Think of it like an insurance surcharge: when you drive in a dangerous neighborhood, your car insurance costs more. Today, oil markets are charging a "war premium" because any escalation near the Strait of Hormuz — where 1 in 5 barrels of global oil passes — could suddenly reduce supply and send prices much higher.
