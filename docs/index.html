  • Brent crude crashing 8.58% — this is actually good news for inflation and consumers, but the speed of the drop signals someone knows something about demand weakening
💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **stagflation risk** — a situation where prices stay high (inflation) at the same time the economy slows down (stagnation), which is the worst of both worlds for investors. Think of it like being stuck in traffic while your gas tank is draining — you're not getting anywhere AND you're losing fuel. Tariffs create stagflation because they raise prices for consumers while simultaneously hurting business profits and economic growth.
