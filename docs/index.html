  • Oil breaking below $70 despite a geopolitical attack — normally, threats to oil shipping send prices UP; the fact that prices fell ANYWAY tells you markets are more worried about weak demand (slowing economy) than supply disruptions, which is a serious warning sign.
💡 Concepto del día

**The Fear Index (VIX):** The VIX measures how much investors expect the stock market to swing up or down over the next 30 days — think of it as the market's "anxiety thermometer." When VIX is above 20, like today, it means investors are genuinely nervous and buying insurance (options) against losses. A good analogy: imagine the VIX is the weather forecast — a reading above 20 is like a 60% chance of storms, not a guarantee of rain, but enough to make you carry an umbrella.
