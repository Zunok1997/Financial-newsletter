💡 Concepto del día

**Bear Steepening** — Today's bond market showed what analysts call a "bear steepening," where long-term interest rates rise faster than short-term rates, making the yield curve steeper but in a painful way. Think of it like a seesaw where the far end (30-year bonds) is getting heavier — it means investors demand more compensation to lend money for longer periods, usually because they're worried about inflation or government debt levels. When this happens aggressively, it tends to squeeze the valuation of expensive growth stocks because the math of discounting future profits gets less favorable.
