💡 Concepto del día

**VIX Term Structure Divergence** — Today's VIX (spot) is 16.64, meaning markets are calm *right now*, but VIX 3-Month is 20.03, meaning smart money is paying up for protection *over the next 3 months*. Think of it like the weather: today is sunny, but the 3-month forecast shows storms — and sophisticated investors are already buying umbrellas. When this gap widens, it's a signal that institutional investors see risks on the horizon that aren't yet visible in daily price action.
