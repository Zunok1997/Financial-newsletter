💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is the **VIX Divergence** — when the stock market goes up but the VIX (the "fear gauge") also rises sharply, it means professional investors are quietly buying insurance (put options) even while prices climb. Think of it like a neighborhood where home prices are rising, but homeowners are suddenly buying more fire insurance — they're optimistic on value but worried about a specific risk they can see coming. Today's 8.5% VIX spike while stocks gained is a classic signal that sophisticated money is hedging, not celebrating.
