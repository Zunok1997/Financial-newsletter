💡 Concepto del día

Today's key concept is the **Real Interest Rate** — the interest rate after subtracting inflation. Think of it like this: if your savings account pays 4.5% but inflation is 4.2%, you're only really earning 0.3% in purchasing power. When real rates are very low or negative, people buy stocks and gold to protect their money; when real rates rise (as they are now), money flows back to safe bonds, which hurts stocks and riskier assets.
