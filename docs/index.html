💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **"Yield Curve Steepening."** Think of the yield curve like a staircase: the short steps are short-term interest rates (like 3-month T-bills at 3.62%) and the long steps are long-term rates (like the 30-year Treasury at 4.975%). When the long steps grow faster than the short ones — as they are today — investors are demanding more compensation to lend money for a long time, usually because they're worried about future inflation or government debt. It's like agreeing to lend a friend money for 30 years instead of 3 months — you'd want a lot more interest for that risk.
