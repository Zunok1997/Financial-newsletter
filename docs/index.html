💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is the **Yield Curve and Its Warning Signal**. The yield curve compares short-term interest rates (3M T-Bill at 3.73%) to long-term rates (30Y Treasury at 5.13%) — when long rates are much higher than short rates, it often signals that bond investors expect persistent inflation and aren't confident the Fed can control it. Think of it like a doctor taking your temperature at two points — if both are running hot but the long-term reading is hotter, the illness isn't going away anytime soon.
