💡 Concepto del día

**VIX Divergence** — Normally, when stocks go up, fear (measured by the VIX) goes down. Today the opposite happened: stocks rose but VIX also rose. Think of it like a party where everyone is dancing, but someone quietly called a taxi — it means some participants don't fully trust the good mood and are preparing for things to go wrong. When this divergence appears, it's a signal to check your portfolio's risk levels rather than chase the rally.
