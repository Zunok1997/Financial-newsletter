💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **Bear Steepening** — this happens when long-term interest rates rise faster than short-term rates, widening the gap between them. Think of it like a seesaw tilting: the long end (30Y at 4.985%) is rising much faster than the short end (3M at 3.668%). This pattern often signals that investors are worried about future inflation or too much government borrowing — and historically, it has been a warning sign that precedes market stress, not a sign of healthy economic growth.
