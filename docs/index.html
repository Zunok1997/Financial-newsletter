💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **"Geopolitical Risk Premium."** When a war-related event happens, investors demand extra compensation for holding risky assets — they want to be paid more to take on uncertainty. Think of it like this: if you were renting out your house in a neighborhood that just had a major incident, you'd charge more rent to justify the risk. Today, oil traders instantly charged more for crude (the rent), gold buyers paid up for safety, and stock investors rotated away from risky tech into stable defense and energy companies.
