📋 Resumen del día

Today's market is telling two stories at once: one of excitement around artificial intelligence (Palantir's stunning quarter) and one of concern about the everyday consumer (McDonald's missing its numbers). Underneath it all, bond yields are rising sharply, gold is breaking records above $4,100, and oil is crashing — a combination that signals investors are nervous about the big picture even as some stocks celebrate.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • Palantir +16% on AI commercial revenue beat — confirms that enterprise AI spending is real and growing, not just hype, which matters for the entire technology sector's valuation story.
  • 30Y Treasury yield at 5.275% — when long-term government borrowing costs rise this high, it squeezes company valuations and makes stocks in general more expensive relative to "safe" bonds.
  • Oil crashing -4.4% to -6% — a sharp oil drop of this size in one day signals that markets are worried about weaker global economic demand, which is a warning sign worth watching carefully.
💡 Concepto del día

**Late-Cycle Bifurcation** means we are in a phase of the economic cycle where winners and losers are splitting apart dramatically — think of it like a race where the front runners (AI companies, gold, quality businesses) are pulling far ahead while the back of the pack (consumer-facing companies, long-term bonds) is slowing down. It's like a restaurant where the VIP section is packed and thriving while the regular dining room is emptying out — both are in the same building, but having very different nights. Recognizing which "section" your investments are in is the most important skill in this environment.
