💡 Concepto del día

**Geopolitical Risk Premium** is the extra price that investors demand — or pay — for assets when wars, conflicts, or political instability could disrupt supply chains, energy flows, or global trade. Think of it like car insurance: you might not expect an accident, but you pay more when you're driving in a storm. Today, that "storm" is U.S.-Iran tensions, and the "insurance premium" shows up as higher oil prices, a stronger gold price, and rising defense stock values — all happening simultaneously as the market hedges against a scenario where the conflict spreads.
