10 Conclusión del Día — Para Principiantes click to expand ›
📋 Resumen del día

Today's markets sold off across the board as two powerful forces hit at the same time: bond yields surged (the 10-year Treasury hit 4.595%) while oil jumped above $102 per barrel due to Trump's warning to Iran. Think of it like a business facing both higher borrowing costs and higher fuel prices on the same day — profits get squeezed from two directions at once, and that's exactly what investors are pricing in right now.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • Rising Treasury yields (10Y at 4.595%) — higher yields make future corporate profits worth less today, directly pushing stock prices down, especially for expensive tech companies
  • Trump's Iran warning pushing oil above $102 WTI — higher oil re-ignites inflation fears, which reduces the chances of Fed rate cuts and compounds the pressure on equities
  • Nasdaq leading declines at -1.54% — the most expensive part of the stock market (AI/tech stocks) is the most vulnerable when interest rates rise, and today's action confirms that rotation
💡 Concepto del día

**Duration Risk** is the sensitivity of an investment's price to changes in interest rates. Think of it like a seesaw: the longer you have to wait for your money back (a 30-year bond, or a tech company whose big profits are 10 years away), the harder you get hit when interest rates go up. Today's selloff in Nasdaq is a perfect real-world example — AI stocks are essentially "long duration" bets on future profits, so when the 10-year yield jumps, those future profits are worth less in today's dollars, and the stock price falls even if the company hasn't changed at all.
