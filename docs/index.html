💡 Concepto del día

**H2 Rebalancing** — At the start of every new half-year, large funds like pension plans and mutual funds automatically buy or sell assets to get back to their target allocations. Think of it like tidying your room: if tech stocks grew so much that they now take up 40% of your portfolio when you wanted 30%, you sell some tech and buy other things. This mechanical buying and selling can create exaggerated moves in the first days of July that don't always reflect real fundamental news — so be careful not to chase momentum blindly this week.

👀 Para mañana
