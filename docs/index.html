💡 Concepto del día

**Bull Flattening** — today, longer-term interest rates (10Y, 30Y) fell while short-term rates held steady, which is called a "bull flattening" of the yield curve. Think of it like a seesaw where the far end is dropping — this usually happens when investors believe economic growth will slow, so they rush into long-term bonds for safety, pushing their yields down. For stock investors, this is generally good news for growth companies (like tech) because their future profits become worth more when long-term rates fall.
