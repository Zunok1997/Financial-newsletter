💡 Concepto del día

**Geopolitical Risk Premium** is the extra return investors demand (or the price spike in commodities) when a political or military event threatens to disrupt supply chains or economic stability. Think of it like a "danger tax" — when a war or attack threatens oil shipping routes, buyers pay more for crude today because they fear there might be less of it tomorrow. Today, WTI crude jumping nearly 3% in a single day is a textbook example of this premium appearing in real time.
