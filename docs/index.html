💡 Concepto del día

**Bear Steepening** — today's yield curve showed the 10-year Treasury yield falling slightly while the 30-year yield rose to nearly 5%, making the curve "steeper" from the long end. Think of it like a seesaw where the far end is going up: it usually means bond investors expect inflation or larger government borrowing in the distant future, even if things look okay today. This matters because it raises the cost of long-term borrowing for everything from mortgages to corporate bonds.
