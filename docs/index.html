**Yield Curve Steepening** means that long-term interest rates (like the 10-year Treasury at 4.39%) are rising faster than short-term rates. Think of it like a bank charging you more for a 30-year mortgage than a 1-year loan — when that gap widens, it usually signals the market expects higher inflation or more government borrowing ahead, which can put pressure on stock valuations especially for growth companies.
