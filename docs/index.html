The result is a fractured market — the Dow holds up while the Nasdaq slides, gold breaks $4,100, and European stocks outperform, painting a picture of investors rotating away from growth and toward hard assets and value.

⭐ Lo más importante

  • Trump's new tariffs on 60 countries — unlike past rounds, this hits almost everyone including Europe, meaning corporate supply chains built to avoid China tariffs are now also at risk, threatening earnings guidance across hundreds of companies
  • Gold breaking above $4,100 — when gold, silver, AND copper all rise together while stocks are uncertain, it's the market's way of saying "we don't trust paper assets right now," which is a yellow flag investors should respect
💡 Concepto del día

**Reflexivity** (George Soros): This is the idea that markets don't just reflect reality — they *change* reality. Today's example: peace talks cause oil to drop → lower oil reduces Iran's oil revenue and financial power → Iran becomes more motivated to reach a deal → which further validates the oil drop. It's a self-reinforcing loop. Think of it like a rumor in high school that becomes true because everyone acts as if it's already true.
