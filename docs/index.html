💡 Concepto del día

**Sector Rotation** is when investors move money out of one group of stocks (like tech) and into another (like industrials or commodities) — not because they're leaving the market, but because they're repositioning for the next phase of the economic cycle. Think of it like a restaurant shifting its menu from summer salads to hearty fall stews — the customers don't leave, they just order differently. Today's Dow outperforming Nasdaq is a textbook example: rising interest rates make future tech profits worth less today, so money flows toward companies that earn profits right now.
