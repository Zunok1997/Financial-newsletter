💡 Concepto del día

**Sector Rotation** is when investors move money from one industry group to another rather than leaving the stock market entirely. Today is a textbook example: money flowed OUT of high-growth technology stocks (Nasdaq -2.21%) but the Dow Jones barely moved (-0.09%) because it holds more "boring" companies like industrials and healthcare that investors ran toward for safety. Think of it like a crowded restaurant — when the trendy section gets too noisy and expensive, some diners quietly move to the quieter, more reliable part of the room without actually leaving the building.
