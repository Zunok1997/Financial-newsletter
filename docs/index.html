⭐ Lo más importante

  • A major satellite TV company filed for bankruptcy — this is a reminder that industries disrupted by technology (streaming vs. cable/satellite) eventually see their weakest players collapse, and this trend is not reversing.
  • Oman is staying deliberately vague about Strait of Hormuz transit fees — this tiny country controls the world's most important oil shipping lane, and its silence is making it impossible for oil traders to price the risk properly, creating hidden danger.
💡 Concepto del día

**Safe Haven Assets** — these are investments that people rush into when they feel nervous or uncertain about the world, because they tend to hold their value or even rise when everything else falls. Gold is the classic example: it doesn't pay dividends or earnings, but humans have trusted it for thousands of years as a store of value. Think of it like keeping cash under the mattress — except the mattress itself becomes more valuable when the house around it is on fire.
