📋 Resumen del día

Today's market sent a complex but important message: while stock indexes look mostly calm on the surface, underneath there is significant stress building. Bond yields jumped sharply (the 10-year Treasury rose nearly 1% in one day), gold and commodities surged, and a major consumer company — PepsiCo — missed earnings because Americans are spending less. This is a market quietly preparing for something bigger.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • 10-Year Treasury yield spiked to 4.57% — higher bond yields make stocks more expensive relative to "safe" returns, which can trigger selloffs in growth stocks.
  • PepsiCo missed earnings on weak North American sales — this is a warning sign that everyday consumers are under real financial pressure, which affects the whole economy.
  • Gold hit $4,108 and copper surged +2.54% — when both precious AND industrial metals rally together, it usually means investors are pricing in inflation, uncertainty, or both simultaneously.
💡 Concepto del día

**Yield and Valuation** — When bond yields rise, it's like raising the "minimum acceptable return" bar for all investments. If a safe government bond now pays 4.57%, why would you accept the same return from a risky stock? You wouldn't — so investors demand stocks be cheaper, which pushes prices down. Think of it like this: if your savings account suddenly offered 4.5% interest, you'd think twice before putting money in something riskier for only a slightly better return.
