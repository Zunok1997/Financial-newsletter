Think of it as a day when both the world news and the corporate news pointed in the same bearish direction, leaving investors with few safe places to hide except gold and defense stocks.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • U.S. strikes on Iran resumed and Kuwait closed its airspace — this matters because any escalation near the Strait of Hormuz could disrupt roughly 20% of the world's oil supply, sending energy prices sharply higher and increasing inflation pressure globally.
  • VIX spot fell today but the 3-month VIX surged over 7% — this matters because it means professional traders are quietly buying protection against something bad happening in the next 60-90 days, even as today's surface calm might trick casual observers into thinking fear is subsiding.
  • Oracle fell despite beating earnings expectations — this matters because it signals that investors are losing patience with AI companies that spend enormous amounts of cash without yet generating it back, a theme that could pressure many high-flying tech stocks in coming months.
💡 Concepto del día

**VIX Term Structure** — The VIX measures how much fear exists in the market *right now*, but the 3-month VIX measures how much fear investors expect *in the future*. Think of it like a weather forecast: today might be sunny (spot VIX falling), but the 90-day forecast shows a major storm coming (3M VIX rising sharply). When the future fear is higher than today's fear, sophisticated investors are quietly buying insurance, which is exactly what we saw today — a hidden warning sign dressed up as a calm day.
