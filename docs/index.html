💡 Concepto del día

**The VIX Term Structure** — Today the spot VIX (measuring fear RIGHT NOW) is 17.11, but the 3-Month VIX is 20.08, meaning the options market is more worried about the next 90 days than about today. Think of it like weather: today is sunny (VIX 17), but the 3-month forecast shows storms ahead (VIX 20). When this "term structure" is upward sloping like this, smart investors maintain portfolio hedges even when today feels calm, because the market is quietly pricing in future turbulence.
