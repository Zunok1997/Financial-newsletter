💡 Concepto del día

**Conglomerate Discount** — when one company owns many very different businesses, the stock market often values the whole thing for less than the individual pieces would be worth separately. Think of it like buying a combo meal that costs less than ordering each item individually, except here, breaking it apart actually *creates* value for shareholders. Comcast's 23% jump today is a textbook example of the market repricing the pieces now that they'll be sold separately.
