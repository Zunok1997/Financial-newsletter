💡 Concepto del día

**VIX vs. VIX 3M Spread** — Today, the regular VIX is 17.90 (calm), but the 3-month VIX is 21.12 (worried). Think of it like weather forecasting: today is partly cloudy, but the 3-month forecast shows a storm coming. When this gap is wide, smart investors use it as a signal to buy insurance (hedges) cheaply today before the storm arrives.
