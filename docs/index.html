10 Conclusión del Día — Para Principiantes click to expand ›
📋 Resumen del día

Today's market told two different stories: the Dow rose while the Nasdaq fell, which is a classic sign that investors are moving money from exciting-but-risky growth stocks into safer, more established companies. The big macro theme driving everything is the Federal Reserve meeting — specifically, the unusual situation where incoming Fed Chair Warsh is expected to skip putting his interest rate forecast in the official "dot plot," creating rare uncertainty about where rates are headed.

⭐ Lo más importante
  • Fed Chair Warsh withholding his rate dot — this matters because markets use the dot plot as a roadmap for future interest rates, and removing one dot (especially the Chair's) is like getting directions with a key street missing.
  • 10-Year Treasury yield rising to 4.487% — this matters because when long-term interest rates rise, it makes borrowing more expensive and reduces the value of future earnings, which is why tech stocks fell harder today.
  • Gold hitting $4,353 — this matters because gold's breakout above all-time highs is signaling that sophisticated investors are worried about something deeper — geopolitical risk, dollar weakness, and inflation concerns all at once.
💡 Concepto del día

**The Yield Curve** is simply the difference between short-term and long-term interest rates. Think of it like a hotel: normally, booking a room for a whole year costs more per night than booking for one night (long-term = higher rate). Today, the 3-month T-Bill yield fell slightly while the 10-year yield rose — this "steepening" is like the long-stay rate getting more expensive relative to the short stay, which typically benefits banks (who borrow short and lend long) but hurts growth stocks.

👀 Para mañana

The FOMC decision and press conference will be the single most important event in the next 24 hours — pay close attention to any language about the timing of rate cuts and whether Warsh speaks clearly about his personal rate outlook despite skipping the dot. Also watch for any Iran deal developments out of the G7 and Bitcoin's ability to hold the $64,000 support level.
