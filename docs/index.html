💡 Concepto del día

**Yield Curve Steepening** — today, the 10-year Treasury yield fell while the 30-year stayed high near 5.19%, making the curve "steeper" at the long end. Think of it like a mortgage: if short-term rates drop but 30-year mortgage rates stay high, it means the market believes inflation or government debt risk will be a problem for a long time, even if today feels calmer. It's a warning sign hidden inside an otherwise bullish day.
