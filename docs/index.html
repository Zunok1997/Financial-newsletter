  • Gold, silver, and copper all rose together — this rare alignment means both fear (gold/silver as safe havens) and optimism (copper as industrial demand indicator) are present simultaneously, a confusing but important signal to track
💡 Concepto del día

Today's concept is **Yield Curve Steepening** — when long-term interest rates (like the 30-year at 5%) rise much faster than short-term rates (like the 3-month T-bill at 3.59%), the yield curve "steepens." Think of it like a restaurant: the short-term rate is the lunch price, and the long-term rate is the dinner price — when dinner suddenly costs much more, it means people expect inflation or financial stress ahead. A steepening curve after a period of inversion is historically associated with late economic cycles and can precede both opportunities and risks in equities.
