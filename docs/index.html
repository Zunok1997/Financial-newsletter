  • New 10% tariffs proposed on 60 economies — this directly threatens supply chains and corporate profit margins across retail, tech, and industrials, making forward earnings guidance unreliable
  • Palo Alto Networks falling after earnings — it's a warning that even good companies can fall hard if their stock price already assumed perfection, and right now many tech stocks are priced that way
💡 Concepto del día

**Geopolitical Risk Premium** — this is the extra price the market charges for uncertainty caused by wars, sanctions, or political instability. Think of it like an insurance surcharge: when you live in a flood zone, your home insurance costs more. Today, oil costs more partly because of Iran uncertainty, not just because of supply and demand. When the risk premium deflates (a deal is signed, tensions ease), prices can drop fast even if nothing physical changed — that's what makes it tricky to trade.
